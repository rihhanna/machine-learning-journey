## What I Learned

### Why Use Multiple Decision Trees?

Instead of relying on one decision tree, we can combine many trees.

This creates a stronger and more accurate model.

The combined model is called an **Ensemble**.

---

### Sampling with Replacement (Bootstrap)

Random Forest creates different training datasets by randomly selecting examples **with replacement**.

This means:

- Some examples may appear multiple times.
- Some examples may not appear at all.

Each tree learns from a different dataset.

---

### Random Forest

A Random Forest is a collection of many decision trees.

Each tree makes its own prediction.

The final prediction is obtained by combining all trees.

Benefits:

- Higher accuracy
- Less overfitting
- Better generalization

---

### XGBoost

XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting) builds trees one after another.

Each new tree focuses on correcting the mistakes made by previous trees.

It is one of the most powerful machine learning algorithms and is widely used in industry and Kaggle competitions.

---

### When to Use Decision Trees

Decision Trees work well when:

- The data has non-linear relationships.
- The features are a mix of numerical and categorical values.
- Interpretability is important.

For better performance, Random Forest and XGBoost are often preferred.

---

## Key Concepts

- Ensemble Learning combines multiple models.
- Bootstrap Sampling creates different datasets.
- Random Forest reduces overfitting.
- XGBoost improves predictions iteratively.
- Ensemble methods usually outperform a single decision tree.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Multiple trees are stronger than one tree.
- Random Forest = Parallel trees.
- XGBoost = Sequential learning.
- Ensemble learning improves accuracy and robustness.
