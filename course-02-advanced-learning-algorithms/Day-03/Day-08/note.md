## What I Learned

### ReLU Activation
ReLU helps neural networks learn complex patterns efficiently and is commonly used in hidden layers.

### Softmax Function
Softmax converts outputs into probabilities that sum to 1, allowing neural networks to classify multiple classes.

### Multiclass Classification
Unlike binary classification, multiclass classification predicts one class from several possible classes.

### Optimization and Derivatives
Optimization techniques improve model training, while derivatives and computation graphs are fundamental concepts behind backpropagation and learning.

---

## Key Takeaways

- ReLU is commonly used in hidden layers.
- Softmax is used for multiclass classification.
- Softmax outputs probabilities that always sum to 1.
- Gradients and derivatives are essential for training neural networks.
- Better optimization methods can improve model performance.
